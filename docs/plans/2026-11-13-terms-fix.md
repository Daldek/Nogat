# Plan: regulamin 1.0 corrected and published again, the other providers' lines

Approved: 2026-11-13, the author (in session), with the national borders of decision 6

Status: done 2026-11-14. Steps 1 and 3 to 6 merged into the `develop` of Nogat, Hydrograf, Hydrolog, Obserwator and Archiwista, and regulamin 1.0 republished with `terms_effective` 2026-11-14 (step 7, [Results](#results)); step 2, conditional, brought no Kartograf commit. No tags, no release.

Requirements: DEV-15.6, DEV-15.7, DEV §20, DEV-12.4 to DEV-12.9 (ECO-066), ECO-062, ECO-065, HUB-5.2 (producers before consumers), DEV-17.5.

## Goal

The regulamin 1.0 published on 2026-11-13 in Hydrograf and Hydrolog says three things that are not true, and its § 5 points to terms that are not the ones that bind us:

| Where | What it says | What is true |
|---|---|---|
| § 5 ust. 1 | the service adds to views, files and results what the providers require | Hydrograf's results and files from GUGiK data (NMT, BDOT10k) and ISRIC SoilGrids carry no source line; GUGiK requires its full name, the time of creation and acquisition and a note that the data were processed ([GUGiK](https://www.gov.pl/web/gugik/ponowne-wykorzystanie-informacji-sektora-publicznego)); CC BY 4.0 requires the creator, the licence and a note of changes |
| § 5, block `gugik` | links „Regulamin geoportalu” | the conditions that bind reuse are GUGiK's conditions of reuse (link above) |
| § 5, blocks `copernicus` and `esri` | Hydrograf uses CORINE and Esri World Imagery | CORINE is not used by Hydrograf's pipeline (`backend/scripts/README.md`, ADR-061); Esri basemaps may be used only with Esri software or an ArcGIS subscription ([Esri](https://www.esri.com/content/dam/arcgisonline/docs/tou_summary.pdf)), which Hydrograf has not |
| § 8 ust. 3 | the access log keeps the User-Agent | ECO-066 keeps only its class (already in the template on develop) |
| § 8 ust. 5 | only the basemap provider receives the visitor's IP address | so do the flood map overlay of wody.isok.gov.pl and the script CDNs unpkg.com and cdn.jsdelivr.net that Hydrograf loads |

The author decided on 2026-11-13: the corrected text keeps the number 1.0 and is published again; the 1.1 bump of the logging branch was dropped before it was pushed.

## Decisions

1. **Version.** The template stays „Wersja 1.0”, history „1.0: pierwsza wersja”. `terms_effective` becomes the day the corrected text is published. `design/README.md` records that 1.0 was published on 2026-11-13 and corrected in place on the author's decision; from the republication on, any change raises the version. The history link points to the template's history on `develop`, where the published text lives; `main` is released only on the author's word.
2. **New lines in DEV §20**, quoted byte for byte like the others:

   | Mark | Line |
   |---|---|
   | G | Źródło: Główny Urząd Geodezji i Kartografii – GUGiK, `<products>` (wytworzono `<period>`, pozyskano `<date>`); dane przetworzone |
   | GK | Źródło: GUGiK (`<products>`) |
   | SG | Źródło: ISRIC – World Soil Information, SoilGrids, licencja CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/); dane przetworzone |
   | SK | Źródło: ISRIC SoilGrids, CC BY 4.0 |

   GK and SK are the strip forms (DEV-20.5), like CK, never written into a product; G and SG go into every product: response, export, image, hand-off. `<products>` names the GUGiK products used (e.g. „NMT, BDOT10k”), `<period>` the years of creation of the data used, `<date>` the day they were downloaded, both from the Kartograf metadata of the files. No terms line for either: the regulamin's § 5 links GUGiK's conditions, and SG carries its licence.
3. **Regulamin text** (1.0, corrected):
   - § 5 `gugik`: „Źródło: Główny Urząd Geodezji i Kartografii – GUGiK” and „Warunki ponownego wykorzystywania danych GUGiK: https://www.gov.pl/web/gugik/ponowne-wykorzystanie-informacji-sektora-publicznego”.
   - § 5 `esri`: removed from the template; no GUI uses Esri.
   - § 5 `copernicus`: stays in the template for a future GUI; Hydrograf drops it.
   - § 8 ust. 5: „Podkład mapowy i warstwy map innych dostawców (§ 5) Twoja przeglądarka pobiera bezpośrednio z ich serwerów, które otrzymują przy tym Twój adres IP.” A GUI loads no script, style or font from another server (new sentence in DEV-15.7).
   - § 8 ust. 3: as ECO-066 worded it.
4. **Hydrograf's orthophoto** is GUGiK's (WMTS `https://mapy.geoportal.gov.pl/wss/service/PZGIK/ORTO/WMTS/StandardResolution`), credited „Ortofotomapa © GUGiK” on the map.
5. **ČÚZK lines in DEV §20**, for the borders of decision 6:

   | Mark | Line |
   |---|---|
   | Z | Źródło: Český úřad zeměměřický a katastrální (ČÚZK), `<products>` (pozyskano `<date>`), licencja CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/); dane przetworzone |
   | ZK | Źródło: ČÚZK, CC BY 4.0 |

   ZK is the strip form, Z goes into products, as G and GK.
6. **Obserwator's borders come from the national registers**, not Natural Earth (the author, 2026-11-13): Poland from PRG (GUGiK, the state border of the national file), Czechia from RÚIAN (ČÚZK, the state), both fetched with `kartograf boundaries download --whole-country`. A build script in Obserwator simplifies them in a metric CRS to a tolerance it documents, writes them in WGS 84 to the two packaged files with `source`, `created`, `acquired`, `license` and the tolerance as properties, and is the only way the files change. They serve as the map's background without a basemap, whose strip then shows GK („Źródło: GUGiK (PRG)”) and ZK, and as the catalog's country check, whose tolerance (`BORDER_TOLERANCE_M`, 1500 m for Natural Earth) is set again from the new outlines and the stations' coordinate uncertainty. Products of the store catalog, whose location reasons come from that check, carry G and Z; the regulamin of Obserwator keeps the blocks `gugik` and `cuzk`.

## Steps

1. **Nogat** (branch `fix/terms-1.0`): DEV §20 with G, GK, SG, SK, Z, ZK and the rules for GUGiK, ISRIC and ČÚZK data in the house style; DEV-15.7 gains the no-remote-resources sentence; ECO-068; the template and its blocks as above, the history link on `develop`, `nogat-terms.html` regenerated; `design/README.md` (version record, block table: `copernicus` none, `esri` removed, `gugik` and `cuzk` for Obserwator); kit 0.6.2; contract tests of the lines and the template.
2. **Kartograf**, only if needed: confirm that `kartograf-meta/1` records the source product, its creation time and the download time for NMT, BDOT10k, PRG and RÚIAN files; if a field is missing, add it first (producer before consumer).
3. **Hydrograf**: Esri replaced by the GUGiK orthophoto; `BLOCKS` without `copernicus` and `esri`; unpkg and jsDelivr files vendored into `frontend/vendor/` and the CSP narrowed; GK and SK in the strip of the map, identify and hydrograph views when they show results from those data; G and SG in every API `attribution`, export, image and hand-off derived from them; the identify panel names „ISRIC SoilGrids” instead of „SoilGrids (Kartograf)”; kit 0.6.2; the logging of ECO-066 adopted in the same release, so the page tells the truth on the day it is published.
4. **Hydrolog**: kit 0.6.2 and the logging of ECO-066.
5. **Obserwator**: the borders of decision 6; kit 0.6.2 and the logging of ECO-066, in the release that switches obserwator-test to the target GUI.
6. **Archiwista**: where it shows a station's location reason from Obserwator's catalog, it carries the catalog's lines.
7. **Republication**, one day: Hydrograf and Hydrolog deployed, `terms_effective` set to that day in `nogat.toml`, both restarted; Obserwator publishes the same text at its switch. Nogat `main` is not touched.

Each project's gate checks that its filled page has every field, no `{{`, and only the blocks it lists; Hydrograf's also that every product from GUGiK or SoilGrids data carries G or SG.

## Not in scope

The admin panel (its own plan and decision, on `feat/admin-panel`), a regulamin for Archiwista (it serves none), and Nogat's `main`.

A GUI passes the full lines; `<nogat-sources>` recognises every G and Z by their fixed text and shows one GK naming the products of all the view's G lines, each once, SK and one ZK, „CC BY 4.0” linked in SK and ZK.
