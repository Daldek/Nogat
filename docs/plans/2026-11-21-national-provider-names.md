Approved: 2026-11-21, the author (in session: „Jest ok” on this plan with its decisions and risks; requested: „mylse, ze bedzie lepiej wszedzie korzystac z nazw narodowych, ale bez znakow diakrytycznych. Teraz mieszamy polska nazwe IMGW z angielska nazwe czeskiego CMHU. Problem musi zostac rozwiaany po stronie Archiwisty jako fix. Na razie zostajemy we wszystkich schematach przy `/1`, bo jest to wciaz wersja rozwojowa”; on the location source and the attribution columns: „B - inaczej. […] wejdzie jako minor juz teraz; C - jak w B”)

Status: approved; step 1 (Nogat) merged into `develop` on 2026-11-21 (`9b12ec6`; decisions 1 for IMGW and 1a at `4d755ee`, `fbbe4d1`); step 5 (Hydrograf) merged (`3a4ecf9`); Obserwator's ČHMÚ part (`a4f98e0`) and Hydrolog's (`b08ae85`) merged; the IMGW id, the `imgw_` slugs and Archiwista's step 4 are on each tool's `chore/round-integration` (2026-11-22).

Requirements: DEV-20.3, DEV-20.5, REL-1.3, REL-1.4, AGI-4.1, ECO-072; Obserwator ADR-001 Q10, ADR-003, ADR-012, ADR-021. Raised by the author's test of Obserwator's station list on 2026-11-21.

## Decisions (the author, 2026-11-21)

1. **National acronyms, without diacritics, in every identifier.** ČHMÚ becomes `CHMU` / `chmu` where the code says the English `CHMI` / `chmi` today: Obserwator's provider id (`--provider CHMU`), its dataset keys `cz.chmu.*`, their slugs `chmu_*` and the store directories named by them (`series/versions/chmu_*`, `stations/versions/chmu_stations`, `analyses/versions/cz.chmu`), message keys, modules, tests; Nogat's registry ids (`chmu`, `chmu-json/1`) and the regulamin's block `chmu`; the consumers' copies of them. IMGW-PIB becomes `IMGW` / `imgw` where an identifier says `IMGW-PIB` / `imgw-pib` (Obserwator's provider id, `--provider IMGW`, Nogat's registry id `imgw`, file names; added by the author later the same day: „IMGW-PIB moze byc skracane do IMGW. Bo znowu jest niekonsekwencja w nazwach plikow”); its keys `pl.imgw.*` stay. Texts for people keep the full names („IMGW-PIB”, „ČHMÚ”, the lines S, P, C, CP, CK), and the provider's own addresses (`opendata.chmi.cz`) stay as they are.
1a. **Every slug and store directory of one provider starts with its acronym** (the author, later the same day: „katalog serii tez do ujednolicenia”): IMGW-PIB's slugs gain `imgw_` (`hydro_daily` → `imgw_hydro_daily`, `meteo_precip_daily` → `imgw_meteo_precip_daily`, `pmaxtp` → `imgw_pmaxtp`, every other `pl.imgw.*` slug alike), so `series/versions/imgw_*` stands beside `series/versions/chmu_*`; its station catalog moves from `stations/versions` itself to `stations/versions/imgw_stations` beside `chmu_stations`. The analyses already sit under the key prefixes `pl.imgw` and `cz.chmu`. An old slug is no input; a data root built before is rebuilt (step 7), and the raw root, whose directories follow the provider's paths, is unchanged.
2. **`location_source` names the dataset the location came from**, its key, for both providers: `api_hydro` → `pl.imgw.hydro_now`, `api_meteo` → `pl.imgw.meteo_now`, `chmi_hydro_now` and `chmi_hydro_meta3` → `cz.chmu.hydro_now` (both files of that dataset; the catalog's `sources` keep naming the file), `chmi_hydro_stations` → `cz.chmu.hydro_stations`, `chmi_meteo_now` → `cz.chmu.meteo_now`, `chmi_meteo_stations` → `cz.chmu.meteo_stations`. The two sources outside any dataset stay: `static` (Obserwator's reference file `hydro_stations_locations.csv`) and `user` (the user's file). `pl.imgw.meteo_details` (GEO-03 Q2, Obserwator `v3.2.0`) will be its own value.
3. **The CSV of a series, of the stations and of an analysis drops `attribution_source` and `attribution_processed`**: their two cells repeated the first two cells of `attribution` (checked on the stations export of 2026-11-21: S and P for IMGW-PIB, C and CP for ČHMÚ). The trailing columns are `attribution` and `terms_notice` alone (DEV-20.5).
4. **Every schema and format stays `/1`, and the change is minor**: Obserwator `3.1.0`, not `4.0.0`. No old name is accepted beside the new one. A raw root's stored sidecars of 3.0.x keep their recorded names and are read under the new ones (Obserwator's IMGW-20 aliases: `cz.chmi.*`, `CHMI`, `chmi-json/1`, the provider `IMGW-PIB`), so the raw root is not downloaded again; every input and everything written uses the new names only (coordinator's clarification, 2026-11-21).
5. **Archiwista takes the change as a fix**, in `0.3.3`.
6. Not in this plan: `pl.imgw.meteo_details` (GEO-03 Q2, approved for Obserwator `v3.2.0`), which gives elevation to 856 IMGW-PIB meteo stations from `hydro-back.imgw.pl/station/meteo/status` (`properties.altitude`).

## Risks, named to the author

- **A user of Obserwator 3.0.x** whose scripts say `--provider CHMI`, `--provider IMGW-PIB`, `cz.chmi.*`, read `location_source` values or the two CSV columns gets a refusal or a missing column from 3.1.0, though its number says compatible (decision 4).
- **Production's data root** holds ČHMÚ under the old names (series 716 MB, analyses 3.6 GB, catalog 2.5 MB in `~/data/obserwator-target/data`): 3.1.0 does not read them. The deploy builds a new data root from the unchanged raw root (its directories follow the provider's paths, no `chmi` in them) and switches only after the check; the move to Hydrolog `0.9.0` rebuilds every analysis there anyway (about 6 h). The old root stays for a rollback.
- Files exported by 3.0.x keep the two columns: Hydrolog still reads them as lines, not data, since such files exist on users' disks (decision 3 removes their writing, not their reading).
