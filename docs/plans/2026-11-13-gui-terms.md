# Plan: one regulamin for every GUI

Approved: 2026-11-13, the author's order in session („wszędzie tam, gdzie jest GUI, potrzebny będzie regulamin. Przygotuj jego wzór na Nogacie, z którego będą korzystać inne projekty”)

Amended: 2026-11-13, the author's nine decisions on the template of step 1 (below), recorded as ECO-062: no user signs in, the personal-data blocks and the retention field give way to fixed rules (DEV-15.8 to DEV-15.12), the processor becomes two fields, and the regulamin becomes version 1.0.

Status: step 1 (Nogat, kit 0.2.0) merged into `develop` 2026-11-13; step 2 (Nogat, regulamin 1.0 in kit 0.3.0) done 2026-11-13 on branch `feat/terms-v1`; steps 3 to 5 in each project's own branch; no merges, tags or releases.

Requirements: DEV-15.7 to DEV-15.12, DEV-18.1, ECO-061 and ECO-062 (added by steps 1 and 2), DEV §20 and ECO-060, DEV-15.4, DEV-15.6, DEV-18.2, DEV-18.4, AGI-6.3, DEV-17.5.

## Goal

Every public GUI, Hydrograf, Hydrolog and Obserwator, which anyone uses without signing in, serves the same regulamin in Polish, filled for the deployment, and links it from every view beside the statement of what the services are. It keeps no personal data it does not need: requests are limited per IP address held in memory only, logs carry no IP address, statistics are anonymous daily totals and „Zgłoś uwagę” has no contact field. Archiwista, the operator's private tool behind Cloudflare Access, serves no terms.

## The template's sections (regulamin 1.0)

The heading: the service's name and „Wersja 1.0, obowiązuje od” the date. 1. General: the terms set the rules of use of a free, non-commercial service at its address; data providers and results; using the service accepts the terms. 2. Operator: a private person outside any gainful or professional activity; contact by e-mail only. 3. Nature: the statement of DEV-15.8; commercial use of IMGW-PIB data needs an agreement with IMGW-PIB; data and results informative, not official products; not the sole basis of a decision; in danger follow IMGW-PIB and the services. 4. Access: public, without sign-in, no e-mail collected; the rate limit; the operator may limit or end the service. 5. Data sources and their terms: the blocks with the DEV §20 lines, unchanged but for „serwis” and „wyniki” in lower case. 6. Your duties: lawful use, the providers' lines kept on reuse, no scraping that loads the service, no circumvention of its protection or entry to its admin parts, no unlawful content. 7. Liability: as is, no guarantee of availability or of correct data, open software, the sentence of decision 8, the providers answer for their data. 8. Personal data: the controller; anonymous daily statistics and no stored IP address; the IP address in memory only (the rate window, the daily hash) under art. 6 ust. 1 lit. f; logs without IP addresses, 14 days; the processor's sentence; basemaps loaded by the browser from their providers; „Zgłoś uwagę”; e-mail to the operator; settings in the browser; no own cookies, no external analytics, no automated decisions; only the IP address is needed; the rights, answered within a month; the complaint to UODO. 9. Software licence. 10. Contact and reports: e-mail or the form, answers as time allows, data requests within a month. 11. Changes: in force on publication, without notice; the history. 12. Final provisions: Polish law; mandatory rights kept. „Historia zmian”: 1.0, and the link to the template's history in Nogat.

The text addresses the user as „Ty” and writes „serwis”, „operator” and „wyniki” in lower case, as the sentences of the decisions do.

### 3. Hydrograf (`develop` `12b2a7e`: static frontend behind nginx and FastAPI, PostgreSQL)

- Regulamin: copy kit 0.3.0 into the frontend; fill `regulamin.html` at the container's start from `nogat.toml` (`[nogat.operator]`, its `[nogat.gui]` entry) with the blocks `pmaxtp`, `gugik`, `copernicus`, `isric`, `wody-polskie`, `osm` and `esri`; nginx serves it beside `index.html`; the menu's `terms`.
- Footer: „Regulamin” and the statement of DEV-15.8 in the map's status area of every view, beside the attribution lines of `frontend/js/attribution.js`.
- Rate limit: nginx already limits per address (`docker/nginx.conf`, `docker/nginx-ssl.conf.template`: `/api/` 10 r/s with a burst of 20, `/api/tiles/` 30 r/s with 200, `/api/admin/` 5 r/s, `/api/feedback` 1 r/min with 5), keyed by `$binary_remote_addr`, which behind Cloudflare is the proxy's address: key by the client's (`real_ip_header CF-Connecting-IP` trusted from the proxy only); pages and `/api/` at DEV-15.9's default, the tiles' own limit named in the README, the form's stricter one kept; add `Retry-After` and the Polish answer, the problem document `rate_limited` on `/api/`, which ends the AGI-6.3 deviation in its `CLAUDE.md`.
- Logs: `log_format hydrograf` writes `$remote_addr`, `$remote_user` and `$http_referer`: drop them (DEV-15.10); nginx logs to `/dev/stdout`, so the log driver keeps 14 days by time (or a file rotated daily, 14 kept); check that the API's structlog lines carry no client address.
- Statistics: DEV-15.11 in the API; nginx serves the HTML, so the counter needs the pages passed through the API or a count of each page's first API call, chosen in Hydrograf's plan; `<data_dir>/hydrograf/stats.jsonl` on a volume.
- „Zgłoś uwagę”: exists (`frontend/js/feedback.js`, `frontend/js/diagnostics.js`, `backend/api/endpoints/feedback.py`, table `feedback` of migration 002): a migration drops `contact` and `user_agent` and replaces `page_url` and `diagnostics` by `context` (view, `station` null, parameters, app version); the form loses the contact field and „Dołącz dane diagnostyczne” and gains the line of DEV-15.12.
- Admin: the page `/admin` and `/api/admin/` (`ADMIN_API_KEY`) also behind Cloudflare Access.

### 4. Hydrolog (`develop`: `hydrolog gui serve`, Starlette, Preact without a build)
