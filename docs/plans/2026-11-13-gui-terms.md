Approved: 2026-11-13, the author's order in session („wszędzie tam, gdzie jest GUI, potrzebny będzie regulamin. Przygotuj jego wzór na Nogacie, z którego będą korzystać inne projekty”)

Amended: 2026-11-13, a legal review of regulamin 1.0 and the operator's decisions on it (below), recorded as ECO-063: the review's shortened text, no IP address in any log (DEV-15.10), the wider processor sentence, three more keys of `[nogat.operator]`; kit 0.4.0, the regulamin still 1.0.

## Goal

Every public GUI, Hydrograf, Hydrolog and Obserwator, which anyone uses without signing in, serves the same regulamin in Polish, filled for the deployment, and links it from every view beside the statement of what the services are. It keeps no personal data it does not need: requests are limited per IP address held in memory only, logs carry no IP address, statistics are anonymous daily totals and „Zgłoś uwagę” has no contact field. Archiwista, the operator's private tool behind Cloudflare Access, serves no terms.

## The review and the operator's decisions on it (2026-11-13)

A legal review of regulamin 1.0 (agent Fable, 2026-11-13) found the text well cut for its purpose and asked for three fixes before publication: nginx and the apps may write the client's address to error logs, which would make „nie zapisuje adresów IP” untrue (RODO art. 5 ust. 1 lit. a, art. 13); Cloudflare processes the whole traffic, not only addresses, and keeps its own security logs; § 3 denied that the IMGW-PIB data shown are IMGW-PIB's. It proposed a text one third shorter with the same twelve sections, fields and blocks. The operator decided:

Browser storage, read in the sources on 2026-11-13: Hydrograf (`develop` `12b2a7e`) uses `localStorage` only (theme, layout, coordinates, the welcome; its admin page keeps its key there); Obserwator (`develop` `9aefb23`) uses neither; Hydrolog (`develop` `14e8f06`) uses `localStorage` in its views and `sessionStorage` only in its admin page (`gui/web/admin/admin.js`, the admin key for the tab), which only the operator opens. Hydrolog's server, however, sets the cookie `hydrolog_gui` (`gui/_app.py`: HttpOnly, SameSite strict, no expiry) for a session whose directory holds the user's uploads and outputs until 12 h idle (`gui/_sessions.py`). That contradicts § 8 ust. 7 („nie ustawia własnych plików cookie”, „serwer wykorzystuje tylko do ich wykonania”), so step 4 removes both or the text changes before Hydrolog serves the page.

## The template's sections (regulamin 1.0)

The heading: the service's name and „Wersja 1.0, obowiązuje od” the date. 1. General: the terms set the rules of use of a free, non-commercial service at its address; data providers and results; using the service accepts the terms. 2. Operator: a private person outside any gainful or professional activity; contact by e-mail only. 3. Nature: the statement of DEV-15.8; not a service of IMGW-PIB, ČHMÚ or another provider, their data shown processed, results informative and no official products; not the sole basis of a decision; in danger follow IMGW-PIB and the services. 4. Access: public, without sign-in; the operator may limit or end the service. 5. Data sources and their terms: the lines added without a guarantee; whoever reuses data answers for the providers' terms; the blocks with the DEV §20 lines, N introduced as a summary („W skrócie…”). 6. Your duties: lawful use, the providers' lines kept on reuse, no scraping that loads the service, no circumvention of its protection, no unlawful content or personal data in reports. 7. Liability: as is and at the user's own risk, no guarantee of availability or of correct data, the sentence of decision 8, the providers answer for their data, no liability for linked sites. 8. Personal data: the controller; no data required and no stored IP address; the IP address in memory only (the rate limit, the daily hash, gone by midnight UTC, daily totals on disk) under art. 6 ust. 1 lit. f; no log with an IP address, 14 days; the processor's sentence with its own security logs; basemaps loaded by the browser from their providers; „Zgłoś uwagę” and e-mails, deleted once handled, the mailbox at `mail_provider`; settings in `localStorage`; no own cookies (the processor's against bots when set), no external analytics; the rights with the art. 11 note, answered within a month; the complaint to UODO. 9. Software licence. 10. Contact and reports: e-mail or the form, answers as time allows, data requests within a month. 11. Changes: in force on publication, without notice; the history. 12. Final provisions: Polish law, the Polish text binding; mandatory rights kept. „Historia zmian”: 1.0, and the link to the template's history in Nogat.

The text addresses the user as „Ty” and writes „serwis”, „operator” and „wyniki” in lower case, as the sentences of the decisions do.

### 3. Hydrograf (`develop` `12b2a7e`: static frontend behind nginx and FastAPI, PostgreSQL)

- Regulamin: copy kit 0.4.0 into the frontend; fill `regulamin.html` at the container's start from `nogat.toml` (`[nogat.operator]`, its `[nogat.gui]` entry) with the blocks `pmaxtp`, `gugik`, `copernicus`, `isric`, `wody-polskie`, `osm` and `esri`; nginx serves it beside `index.html`; the menu's `terms`.
- Footer: „Regulamin” and the statement of DEV-15.8 in the map's status area of every view, beside the attribution lines of `frontend/js/attribution.js`.
- Rate limit: nginx already limits per address (`docker/nginx.conf`, `docker/nginx-ssl.conf.template`: `/api/` 10 r/s with a burst of 20, `/api/tiles/` 30 r/s with 200, `/api/admin/` 5 r/s, `/api/feedback` 1 r/min with 5), keyed by `$binary_remote_addr`, which behind Cloudflare is the proxy's address: key by the client's (`real_ip_header CF-Connecting-IP` trusted from the proxy only); pages and `/api/` at DEV-15.9's default, the tiles' own limit named in the README, the form's stricter one kept; add `Retry-After` and the Polish answer, the problem document `rate_limited` on `/api/`, which ends the AGI-6.3 deviation in its `CLAUDE.md`.
- Logs: `log_format hydrograf` writes `$remote_addr`, `$remote_user` and `$http_referer`: drop them (DEV-15.10); nginx names the client in every error of a request, its `limit_req` rejections included, so `limit_req_log_level` and `limit_conn_log_level` go below the `error_log` level and that level to `crit`, or a filter drops the address; nginx logs to `/dev/stdout`, so the log driver keeps 14 days by time (or a file rotated daily, 14 kept); the API's structlog lines and tracebacks carry no client address.
- Statistics: DEV-15.11 in the API; nginx serves the HTML, so the counter needs the pages passed through the API or a count of each page's first API call, chosen in Hydrograf's plan; `<data_dir>/hydrograf/stats.jsonl` on a volume.
- „Zgłoś uwagę”: exists (`frontend/js/feedback.js`, `frontend/js/diagnostics.js`, `backend/api/endpoints/feedback.py`, table `feedback` of migration 002): a migration drops `contact` and `user_agent` and replaces `page_url` and `diagnostics` by `context` (view, `station` null, parameters, app version); the form loses the contact field and „Dołącz dane diagnostyczne” and gains the line of DEV-15.12.
- Admin: the page `/admin` and `/api/admin/` (`ADMIN_API_KEY`) also behind Cloudflare Access.
