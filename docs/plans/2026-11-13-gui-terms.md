# Plan: one regulamin for every GUI

Approved: 2026-11-13, the author's order in session („wszędzie tam, gdzie jest GUI, potrzebny będzie regulamin. Przygotuj jego wzór na Nogacie, z którego będą korzystać inne projekty”)

Status: step 1 (Nogat) done 2026-11-13 on branch `feat/gui-terms`, built on `fix/provider-attribution` ([provider attribution](2026-11-13-provider-attribution.md)); steps 2 to 5 in each project's own branch; no merges, tags or releases.

Requirements: DEV-15.7, DEV-18.1 and ECO-061 (added by step 1), DEV §20 and ECO-060, DEV-15.4, DEV-15.6, DEV-17.5.

## Goal

Every GUI that a deployment opens to other people serves the same regulamin in Polish, filled for that deployment, and links it from every view. The service is free, non-commercial, without advertising and run by a private person.

## Decisions

| Question | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Where the template lives | `design/kit/` as `nogat-terms.md` (source), `nogat-terms.html` (generated by `ecosystem.py standard --write`, checked by the gate) and `nogat-terms.css`; kit 0.2.0 | copied byte for byte and reported by `drift` like the rest of the kit (DEV-15.6) |
| How it is filled | by the GUI, on the server, at start or at build: blocks first, then `{{field}}` values HTML-escaped; reference `fill` in `scripts/terms.py` | a legal page must read without a script; the algorithm is two steps any project writes in a few lines |
| Where the values come from | `[nogat.operator]` (`name`, `email`, `terms_effective`, `log_retention_days`, `cloudflare_access`) and `[nogat.gui]` of `nogat.toml`; the project's own name, licence, repository and paths | deployment facts sit with the deployment (DEV-18.1); project facts with the project |
| Optional parts | blocks named for data sources (`imgw`, `imgw-hydro`, `pmaxtp`, `chmi`, `gugik`, `cuzk`, `copernicus`, `isric`, `wody-polskie`, `osm`, `esri`) and for personal data (`cloudflare-access`, `accounts`, `session-cookie`, `feedback`); `imgw-terms` and `no-cookies` follow from the others | one list per project, no per-project text |
| Where the link goes | „Regulamin” in the footer or status bar of every view, beside the attribution lines; also at the end of the app menu through its `terms` attribute | the menu is not drawn without another GUI, so it cannot be the only link |
| Address | `regulamin.html` beside the GUI's start page | one stable address in every GUI |
| No `[nogat.operator]` | no page and no link | a local, private run is not a service offered to others |

## The template's sections

1. Definitions. 2. Operator (name and e-mail fields). 3. Purpose and nature: free, non-commercial, no ads, research and education; Data and Results informative, not official products of IMGW-PIB or ČHMÚ, not a sole basis for decisions; in danger follow IMGW-PIB and the services. 4. Access: a browser with JavaScript; Cloudflare Access (block); team accounts (block); the operator may limit or end access. 5. Data sources and their terms: the DEV §20 lines verbatim per source with the address of each provider's terms, the IMGW-PIB terms notice N, operational hydro.imgw.pl data shown live and never archived, ČHMÚ under CC BY 4.0, GUGiK, ČÚZK, Copernicus, ISRIC, Wody Polskie, OpenStreetMap and OpenFreeMap, Esri; reuse binds the user to the providers' terms. 6. User duties: lawful use, attribution kept on reuse, no scraping that overloads the service, no circumvention, no unlawful content. 7. Liability: as is, open software that may contain errors, no liability for decisions or damage within the law except intentional damage, the providers answer for their data (CC BY 4.0 §5). 8. Personal data (RODO): controller, server logs (art. 6(1)(f), retention field), Cloudflare Access with its cookie and transfers (block), team accounts (art. 6(1)(b), block), „Zgłoś uwagę” (block), browser storage only for settings and inputs, own cookies (session cookie block, or none), no analytics or profiling, rights under art. 15 to 18, 20 and 21, complaint to the President of UODO. 9. Software licence (field) and the source code address. 10. Complaints and contact, answer within 30 days. 11. Changes. 12. Effective date, template version, Polish law, consumer rights kept.

## Steps

1. Nogat: the template, `scripts/terms.py`, the menu's `terms` attribute, kit 0.2.0, DEV-15.7, the `operator` table in DEV-18.1, ECO-061, contract, unit and browser tests.
2. Hydrograf: copy the kit; fill and serve `regulamin.html` (its frontend is static behind FastAPI and nginx); blocks `pmaxtp`, `gugik`, `copernicus`, `isric`, `wody-polskie`, `osm`, `esri`, `feedback`; the link in the map's status area and in the menu.
3. Hydrolog: copy the kit; `hydrolog gui serve` fills and serves the page; blocks `pmaxtp`, `feedback`, and `imgw`, `chmi` where the GUI shows station series; the link in the top bar or footer of each section.
4. Archiwista: copy the kit; blocks `imgw`, `imgw-hydro`, `pmaxtp`, `chmi`, `gugik`, `cuzk`, `copernicus`, `isric`, `osm`, `accounts`, `session-cookie`, `feedback`; check whether any other cookie is set; the link in the shell's footer.
5. Obserwator: copy the kit; its public pages serve the page from the template of `base.html`'s app; blocks `imgw`, `imgw-hydro`, `pmaxtp`, `chmi`, and `osm` with a map; the link in the footer of `base.html`.

Each project adds `cloudflare-access` when its deployment sets `cloudflare_access = true`, checks the block list against what its GUI actually shows and processes, and records the step in its own CHANGELOG and PROGRESS.

## For the author before a deployment is opened

- The operator's name and the contact e-mail published on every page.
- The retention of server access logs, of Cloudflare Access logs and of feedback reports; Archiwista's action log keeps 365 days by default.
- Whether the operator's postal address must be published as well (RODO art. 13 names identity and contact details).
- Whether Cloudflare's processing is covered by an agreement on the operator's account.
- The commercial-use question stays open with the author (ECO-060); the template describes a non-commercial service.
