# Plan: the regulamin link in the menu, the rail and the welcome window

Approved: 2026-11-14, the author (in session)

Status: step 1 (Nogat) done 2026-11-14 on branch `feat/no-footer`; steps 2 to 4 in each project's own branch `feat/no-footer`; no merges, pushes or deployments.

Requirements: DEV-15.7 and DEV-15.8 as amended by ECO-070, DEV-15.13, DEV-20.5, DEV-15.6, HUB-5.2 (producers before consumers), DEV-17.5.

## The author's decision (2026-11-14)

The footer statement of DEV-15.8 („Serwis jest niekomercyjny i bezpłatny… zgodnie z regulaminami IMGW-PIB.” beside „Regulamin”) goes away: it takes space and the link is easy to reach elsewhere. The statement stays as the opening of § 3 of the regulamin; its text does not change and its version stays 1.0.

## Goal

A public GUI (Hydrograf, Hydrolog, Obserwator) has no footer and no status-bar statement, and links „Regulamin” in exactly these places, each in a new tab (`target="_blank"`, `rel="noopener"`):

| Place | How |
|---|---|
| The kit's app menu „Aplikacje” | its `terms` attribute, unchanged |
| The foot of the left navigation rail, or of the left navigation where the GUI has no rail | a small, quiet link `.rail-terms` in the rail's muted text (`design/README.md`, Layout); on a phone, where the rail becomes a bottom bar or sheet, its last small item |
| The welcome window, where the GUI has one | the „Regulamin serwisu” that `fillSources()` writes when given `terms`; no second link |

A GUI without `[nogat.operator]` has neither page nor link, and the admin page (DEV-15.13) none. Untouched: the strip `<nogat-sources>` and its dialog „Źródła i warunki” with „Regulamin serwisu”, since § 5 of the IMGW-PIB terms requires the provider lines on screen; the kit's files and regulamin 1.0.

## Steps

1. Nogat (done): DEV-15.7 lists the three places, DEV-15.8 keeps the statement in § 3 only, DEV-15.13 and DEV-20.5 lose their footer; ECO-070 amends ECO-061, ECO-062 and ECO-064; `design/README.md` describes `.rail-terms` for the rail and the phone bar, the welcome window's link and § 3; the specimen shows the menu's link and the rail's; both mockups link „Regulamin” from the app menu and the rail's foot; a browser test pins the welcome window's link (present in a new tab with `terms`, absent without); the § 3 contract test no longer names a footer.
2. Hydrograf: remove the footer statement and the footer's „Regulamin”; add `.rail-terms` at the rail's foot; keep `terms` on the app menu and on `fillSources()` of its welcome window; tests: the link present in each place, in a new tab, absent without `[nogat.operator]`.
3. Hydrolog: the same as Hydrograf.
4. Obserwator: the same; its left panel becomes a bottom sheet on phones, where the link is the sheet's last small item.

Archiwista serves no regulamin (ECO-062) and has no step.
