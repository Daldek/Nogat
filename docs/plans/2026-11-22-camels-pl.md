## The source (checked on 2026-11-22)

CAMELS-PL 2.1.0, Zenodo record 23275336 (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23275336, 2026-10-11; Brzezińska, Marcinkowski, Dolich, Maharjan, Perz, Piniewski, Kardel, Loritz, Wrzesiński), one ZIP of 1.67 GB (4.9 GB unpacked, 2795 files, MD5 `05d4c1884d535c78299477ac8a00703a`) and a PDF description. Kept on the NAS, `/mnt/magazyn/agent-tmp/camels-pl/`.

- `CAMELS_PL_catchment_boundaries/catchments/CAMELS_PL_catchments.gpkg` (and `.shp`): 869 polygons, EPSG:2180, fields `gauge_id` (IMGW-PIB's nine-digit code), `gauge_name`, `water_body_name`; 845 polygons and 24 multipolygons, 3 invalid geometries; 0.1 km² (Czarna Wieś, Kanał Kuwasy) to 193,666 km² (Tczew, Wisła, beyond Poland's border); nested; 5.9 million vertices, 96 MB. Polygon areas equal `area_metadata` (median ratio 0.999). `gauging_stations.gpkg`: the 869 points.
- The author: the boundaries were made to IMGW-PIB's order, so they are the source of truth for a gauge's catchment.
- Every one of the 869 gauges is in Obserwator's `pl.imgw.hydro_daily`; on nine gauges compared day by day, Q and H are equal to Obserwator's on every day both hold.
- Daily catchment means of E-OBS v33.0e (precipitation min/mean/max/sd, temperatures, wind, humidity, radiation, pressure; 1951-01-01 to 2025-10-31), G2DC-PL+ (to 2019), PET (Hargreaves; Penman–Monteith to 2019), nine attribute files, hydrological signatures, LSTM and HBV simulations and parameters.
- Licence: CC BY 4.0 for the dataset, except „Catchment-aggregated meteorological time series derived from E-OBS are excluded from CC BY 4.0 and are subject to the ECA&D/E-OBS Data Policy” (found by the author, 2026-11-22). That policy (rev. 1, CDS `licence-to-use-E-OBS-products`, 1 b) allows use „strictly for use in non-commercial research and education projects only” and asks that scientific results be submitted for publication without delay; it is not CC BY-NC. Since 2026-11-04 C3S also publishes E-OBS_EU (the EU with Norway, Switzerland, Iceland and the UK, so Poland) under CC BY 4.0, commercial use included. The catchment boundaries, the attributes and the signatures are CC BY 4.0.

## Decisions of the author (2026-11-22)

1. CAMELS-PL's catchment boundaries are the boundaries of the gauges (source of truth).
2. Obserwator shows a gauge's catchment and lets one download it; the catchments of the gauges upstream of it are faintly visible inside it, those downstream are not drawn.
3. A catchment carries both citations: CAMELS-PL's and S and P of IMGW-PIB.
4. The forcing for simulations is Hydrolog's to use, later, by the author; Hydrograf's once it covers all of Poland.
5. The signatures serve as test cases.
6. Kartograf, not Obserwator, fetches the geometry: the catchment boundaries and the gauges' points are geodata (its group `hydro`); Obserwator reads them from Kartograf's store (the author asked „Czy to nie on powinien być odpowiedzialny za pobieranie tych danych?”, then „A co z pozostałymi danymi z CAMELS? Myślę, że warto rozważyć pobieranie całości i jakoś rozdzielić te w magazynie […] może trzeba rozdzielić to pomiędzy Kartografa i Obserwatora?”).
7. All the rest of CAMELS-PL is Obserwator's, under a provider `CAMELS` with keys `pl.camels.*` („1 i 2 zgoda”).
8. Series derived from E-OBS are in the store, the CLI and Python, off by default in the GUI and REST and off on an operator's instance unless the operator turns them on („1 i 2 zgoda”).
9. Obserwator's release dedicated to CAMELS-PL is `v3.4.0` (the author first named `v3.3.0` — „Całe CAMELS-PL wchodzi do `3.3.0` i będzie to tag dedykowany dla zmian związanych z tym źródłem, wszystko pozostałe wejdzie najwcześniej jako 3.4.0, chyba że znajdzie się gruby błąd” — then moved it: „CAMELS przesuwa się na 3.4.0, a Gdańskie Wody wchodzą jako v3.3.0”).

## Design

- **Versions:** each tool knows the CAMELS-PL releases it supports (record id, version, DOI, checksums of the members it reads) and takes the newest known by default, another by `--version`; the concept DOI only tells that a newer release exists (a notice), never adopted unchecked. A new release enters by a patch release of each tool after its layout is checked; members are found by their path pattern, not by the root folder, and a layout that differs more is refused with the version named, nothing written.
- **Kartograf (geometry):** a product of the group `hydro`: the two geopackages (catchments and gauges) read from the ZIP by HTTP range requests (the whole ZIP as fallback), kept as published (DEV-19.5); a new store version only when their bytes change (2.0.0 → 2.1.0 would make none); the sidecar names every CAMELS-PL release with these bytes and the lines CAMELS-PL, S and P, and the terms line RD (ECO-088).
- **Obserwator (everything else, provider `CAMELS`):** the raw root keeps the ZIP of each supported release whole, as published; built from it:
  - `pl.camels.forcing_eobs_daily`: the E-OBS catchment means (licence: the E-OBS data policy, in the sidecar and the lines; decision 8);
  - `pl.camels.forcing_g2dc_daily`: the G2DC-PL+ catchment means (its licence to be checked);
  - `pl.camels.simulations_daily`: PET (Hargreaves, Penman–Monteith) and the LSTM and HBV simulations, marked as model output;
  - `pl.camels.attributes`: the nine attribute files, joined to the station catalog by the gauge code;
  - observed Q, H and specific discharge are IMGW-PIB's data Obserwator already holds (equal on nine gauges): not built again; LSTM weights and HBV parameters stay in the raw ZIP for Hydrolog.
- **Catchments in Obserwator:** read from Kartograf's store (an optional dependency, a new edge in Nogat's registry); built per gauge in the data root: polygon in WGS84 at full resolution, a simplified one for the map, area, upstream gauges (a gauge whose catchment lies within this one, overlap ≥ 99 %), the CAMELS-PL release they come from; the 3 invalid geometries repaired with a notice. Geometry is not a statistic (ECO-059 stays).
- **GUI („Stacje”):** a chosen gauge's catchment (outline, light fill), the upstream ones faint (thin outline, no fill), nothing downstream; the panel gives the area, the upstream gauges as links and „Granice zlewni: CAMELS-PL <version>”; a gauge outside CAMELS-PL says it has none. The station panel shows the gauge's CAMELS attributes.
- **Downloads (DEV-19.21):** `obserwator_catchment_<code>.geojson`/`.gpkg` with the lines; all catchments via CLI/REST/MCP; the `pl.camels.*` series as other series (E-OBS ones only where decision 8 allows).
- **Attribution:** CAMELS-PL's citation of the exact release (DOI, CC BY 4.0) with S and P of IMGW-PIB and the terms line RD (ECO-088) on the catchments and on the attributes derived from IMGW-PIB's data; the E-OBS citation and policy on `forcing_eobs_daily`; the G2DC-PL+ citation on `forcing_g2dc_daily`; a Nogat ECO decision and DEV §20 lines; the regulamin's block `camels-pl`.
- **Operator's instance:** catchments follow the IMGW filter and the area clip of ADR-025; the provider `CAMELS` is a provider of the filter.

## Results

- Step 1 (2026-11-23, Nogat `feat/camels-pl-and-provider-lines`): ECO-086 records CAMELS-PL as a source and the author's decisions above, with the sources checked that day: the record's licence `cc-by-4.0` and its exclusion of the E-OBS series; the data description of 2.1.0, which lists both CC BY 4.0 and CC BY-NC 4.0 under „1 License” without saying what each covers (the record's licence is taken); the ECA&D/E-OBS data policy (`https://www.ecad.eu/documents/ECAD_datapolicy.pdf`, updated 2019-09-16, the same terms as the CDS „E-OBS product licence (rev. 1)”): „strictly for use in non-commercial research and education projects only” (1 b) and the acknowledgement of E-OBS (2 d); G2DC-PL+ is CC0 1.0 by its 4TU.ResearchData record (DOI `10.4121/uuid:a3bed3b8-e22a-4b68-8d75-7b87109c9feb`, DataCite `cc0-1.0`, the 4TU API „CC0”), which its paper (ESSD 13, 1273–1288, 2021) names as the data's home. DEV §20 gains L and LP („Źródło danych: CAMELS-PL `<version>` (Brzezińska i in., `<year>`), https://doi.org/`<doi>`, licencja CC BY 4.0 (…)”, „Dane CAMELS-PL zostały przetworzone”), E (the E-OBS acknowledgement in its English words), D (G2DC-PL+, CC0 1.0) and the terms line RE (the E-OBS policy), in the table of provider lines after CP (ECO-087); DEV-20.7 links RE and DEV-20.12 says what carries them, the E-OBS series served by a GUI or REST API only where the deployment turns them on (decision 8). CMP-6.10 pins the reference cases of a published data set to its release. The regulamin's template and the operator's draft gain the blocks `camels-pl` and `e-obs` in place (regulamin 1.0), and the kit becomes `1.2.0` (REL-1.4). `ecosystem.yaml`: the data source `camels-pl`, Kartograf's planned product `kartograf-camels-pl`, Obserwator's planned provider `CAMELS` (`obserwator-data/1`) and the planned optional edge Obserwator → Kartograf; `docs/ECOSYSTEM_MAP.md` regenerated. Beyond the step's wording: the block `e-obs`, since a deployment that turns the E-OBS series on needs their terms in its regulamin and a later block would need another kit minor.
- Step 2 (2026-11-23, Kartograf `0.9.0.dev0`, its ADR-062, merged into its `develop`, gate 8800 passed): `hydro download --product camels --country pl --whole-country [--version 2.0.0|2.1.0]`; 1.0.0 refused (another file, CC BY-NC 4.0); members read by HTTP range requests, the whole ZIP as fallback; a ZIP that differs from the pinned size, CRC and SHA-256 is `data_error`/`release_layout` with nothing written; 2.0.0 and 2.1.0 share one store version labelled `2026-10-07`; S, P and L in the sidecar and in the `ATTRIBUTION.txt` of a plain write; `kartograf.testing.build_camels_store` for Obserwator. Live: 2.1.0 from Zenodo in 7 responses, 55.5 MB of the 1.67 GB ZIP, both files byte-equal to the NAS copy.
- Step 5 (2026-11-23, Hydrolog `0.10.0.dev0`, its ADR-0073, merged into its `develop`, gate 18677 passed): CAMELS-PL ships no code; its method is CAMELS-DE's with the hydrological year from 1 November, nearest-rank quantiles and complete years for the FDC slope. New specs `fdc_slope`, `high_flow_days`, `low_flow_days`, `zero_flow_fraction`, `half_flow_date`; 104 cases on 13 gauges (6 to 193,728 km²) pinned to 2.1.0 by SHA-256 (CMP-6.10) match within stated tolerances. BFI does not match: CAMELS-PL uses a two-pass filter with clipped quickflow, Hydrolog keeps Ladson et al.'s three passes (−0.045 to +0.027 on the 13 gauges, up to 0.19 over 869); recorded as a discrepancy (CMP-5.2). Reporting it to CAMELS-PL's authors is the author's call.
- The author's decisions of 2026-11-24 (in session: „1. na razie nic z tym nie robimy, bo i tak nie rozumiem jak to dziala; 2. ok; 3. przyjmujemy wg tego jak jest na Zendoo dla 2.1.0”): (1) BFI's discrepancy is not reported to CAMELS-PL's authors for now, and Hydrolog keeps Ladson et al.'s three passes (its ADR-0073); (2) what CAMELS-PL took from IMGW-PIB (discharge, water levels, catchment boundaries) and what is derived from it carry the terms line RD as well as S and P (Nogat `docs/camels-pl-terms`: ECO-088, DEV-20.7, DEV-20.12), so steps 3 and 4 carry RD on these data from the start, and Kartograf adds it to its CAMELS-PL sidecar and the `ATTRIBUTION.txt` of a plain write on its branch `fix/camels-terms-line` (a follow-up of step 2); (3) CAMELS-PL's licence is that of the Zenodo record of 2.1.0 (CC BY 4.0, the E-OBS catchment series excluded and under the ECA&D/E-OBS data policy), and the data description's listing of CC BY-NC 4.0 under „1 License” is disregarded.
