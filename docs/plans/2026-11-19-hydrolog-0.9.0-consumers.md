# Plan: the consumers of Hydrolog 0.9.0

Approved: pending (the author asked on 2026-11-18 to continue the work on Hydrolog 0.9.0; its release waits for his approval)

Status: steps 1 and 3 done on 2026-11-21 (Hydrolog `develop` at `0.9.0.dev5`, `de3b606`, full gate passed; Obserwator `develop` `55dd035`, full gate passed); steps 2, 4, 5, 6 and 7 open. Nothing released: the round's releases wait for its end and the author's approval.

Requirements: REL-1.1, REL-5.3, REL-5.6, REL-6.6, CMP-2.8, CMP-3.3, CMP-9.2.

## Goal

Every member that calls Hydrolog or stores its results moves to 0.9.0 in the same release set, with the results it stores recomputed and its texts saying what Hydrolog now computes. Producers first: Hydrolog's release, then Hydrograf, Obserwator, Hydraulik and Retman, then Archiwista's schema copy and Nogat's release set.

## What changes in Hydrolog 0.9.0 (from its CHANGELOG `[Unreleased]`)

| Area | Change | ADR |
|---|---|---|
| Euler II | built from a depth–duration curve (`depth_duration_mm` / `depth_duration_file`); `peak_position` removed | 0042 |
| Low flows | Fisher–Tippett quantiles below zero are `null` with the notice `negative_quantiles`; at least 4 complete years | — |
| Hyetographs | a duration that is not a whole number of steps is refused (`not_multiple`); exact Beta and triangular blocks; `duration_not_covered` removed | 0046 |
| Time of concentration | Kirpich 0.0078 for feet with the exact foot (−0.136 %), NRCS exact constant (−0.095 %), Kirpich's applicability 0.107–1.22 km and 0.025–0.1 | 0052 |
| Lutz | Jg is the equal-area slope of the longest flow path's profile, new required field `flow_path_equal_area_slope_m_per_m`; U + W ≤ 100 | 0053 |
| Snyder in the GUI | L is the longest flow path to the divide | 0043 |
| SCS-CN | TR-55 table as data; row crops and small grain without a condition are refused (no invented `fair` rows); CN is a float | 0051 |
| Meteo | normals from monthly normals (WMO-No. 1203 §4.3.3); spells in the year they end; `freeze_thaw_days`; hydrothermal season April–September | 0044 |
| Statistics | exact Mann–Kendall p below 10 values (`p_value_method`), exact K–S p with ties, monthly CI on yearly means (`single_year_month`), Anderson–Darling critical values by parametric bootstrap | 0048, 0049 |
| Every command | `formula_versions` in the envelope; refusals with the standard's `details.reason` (`outside_physical_range`, `invalid_type`, `precondition_failed` + `details.constraint`); the notice `outside_unverified_range` for ranges without a published source | 0050, 0058 |
| Pending | the notice `outside_typical_range` renamed `outside_applicability` (CMP-3.3) | — |

## Steps

1. **Hydrolog**: merge the remaining family branches; rename `outside_typical_range` to `outside_applicability` in the same minor; the release pull request of 0.9.0 (REL-2.1) after the author's approval.
2. **Hydrograf** (pins Hydrolog `v0.8.1` today):
   - compute and send `flow_path_equal_area_slope_m_per_m` (S = 2A/L² of the profile of the same D8 path as `flow_path_length_km`, A the trapezoid area above the outlet; `hydrolog.morphometry.equal_area_slope`);
   - its own Lutz estimate reads `flow_path_length_km` and the equal-area slope, not the channel's; Snyder's L reads `flow_path_length_km`;
   - pass the PMAXTP depth–duration curve for Euler II and fix its labels („DVWK (Euler II)”);
   - durations that are whole multiples of the time step;
   - the TR-55 condition explicit where TR-55 has no `fair` row;
   - the standard's refusal reasons and the notices `outside_unverified_range`, `negative_quantiles`, then `outside_applicability`;
   - store `formula_versions` with its results; HI classes 0.35/0.60 in `docs/COMPUTATION_PIPELINE.md`;
   - its copy of `handoff-1.schema.json` from Nogat `2bbb507`.
3. **Obserwator** (computes its analyses with Hydrolog `0.9.0.dev0` on :8060 since 2026-11-19):
   - recompute every analysis with each new Hydrolog (its carry rule compares Hydrolog's version, so `develop` raises N of `devN` with each change consumers compute with);
   - GUI texts: FD is „Dni z przymrozkiem (Tmin < 0 °C)”, not „Dni przymrozkowe”, also the trend label of `mann_kendall.temperature.frost_days`; optionally `freeze_thaw_days` as „Dni przymrozkowe”; spells over 1 January count in the year they end (`max_duration_days` may be `null`); the hydrothermal season is April–September days above 10 °C; the monthly CI may be `null` (`single_year_month`); Mann–Kendall `p_value_method`; Anderson–Darling critical values `null` for methods other than L-moments; failed fits come as `validation_error` with `precondition_failed` instead of `calculation_error`;
   - store `formula_versions` of every analysis (plan `2026-11-18-computation-standard.md` step 6).
4. **Hydraulik**: `rainfall build` with durations that are whole multiples of the step and the PMAXTP curve for Euler II; findings T36 and T5 on `feature/sfincs-swmm`.
5. **Retman**: the refusal reasons and `formula_versions` in the envelopes it shows; Lutz's new required field in its plans.
6. **Archiwista**: the copy of `handoff-1.schema.json` (example result only).
7. **Nogat**: a release-set candidate with Hydrolog 0.9.0 and the consumers above (REL-6.5).
