## What exists (checked on 2026-11-24)

**Intervals Hydrolog already gives, and Obserwator shows:**

- The long-term mean of a calendar month: spec `mean_interval_half_width` (`statistics/characteristic.py:1562`), Student's t on the k yearly means of the month (batch means, ADR-0049; `characteristic.py:1776-1780`), fields `ci_lower`, `ci_upper`, `confidence_level` of `stats monthly`; Obserwator stores `monthly.Q` with `confidence_level` 0.95 (`store/analysis_registry.py:837-852`), draws it as a `band` („przedział ufności {level} %”, `gui/web/src/sections/hydro/regime.js:79`, `messages/gui/hydro_regime.py:305-307`) and gives the column „PU {level} %” as „dolna–górna” (`regime.js:228-233`).
- Sen's slope: spec `sen_interval` (`statistics/stationarity.py:1183`), Gilbert's interval, `alpha` 0.05 (Obserwator `analysis_registry.py:609`), limits `null` when their ranks fall outside the slopes (`stationarity.py:1457-1458`); Obserwator gives it in the trends table as „slope (dolna … górna)” (`sections/hydro/trends.js:98-106`), not on the chart.

**Point estimates without an interval:**

- Flood quantiles Q_p, `stats flood-frequency` (`statistics/high_flows.py`, `frequency.py`): four distributions (log-normal, GEV, Pearson III, Weibull) by four methods (`l_moments` by default, `mle`, `moments`, `least_squares`; `high_flows.py:71-86`); `FrequencyAnalysisResult` carries `quantiles` and no bounds (`high_flows.py:99-153`). Hydrolog's handbook says so: „Hydrolog tej poprawki ani przedziału ufności nie liczy” (`docs/podrecznik/07-przeplywy-maksymalne.md:1081-1085`), after the regulation (Dz.U. 2026 poz. 692, annex 3, note 3: (1 + δ)Q_k with δ at confidence 0.84 for mountain rivers) and KZGW 2017, which gives the upper limit of a one-sided interval.
- Precipitation quantiles P_max,p: the same command, run by Obserwator as `precipitation_quantiles[.2d|.3d|.5d|.10d]` (`analysis_registry.py:1030-1063`) and shown in „Opady” beside PMAXTP (`sections/meteo/opady.js:1-18`), whose upper bounds of the confidence interval of the quantiles Obserwator already shows („Górna granica”, `messages/gui/pmaxtp.py:92-95`).
- Low-flow quantiles NQ_T, `stats low-flow`: the GEV of the negated annual minima by maximum likelihood (`statistics/low_flows.py:739-760`, `:1008`); a quantile below 0 is `null` with the notice `negative_quantiles`.
- The flow-duration curve, `stats duration-curve`: the period-of-record curve, eq. (3) of Vogel and Fennessey (1994) (`statistics/duration.py:7-9`); its spec says „its confidence intervals (Vogel and Fennessey, sect. 5) are not computed” (`duration.py:457-458`).
- The characteristic flows SSQ, SWQ, SNQ (`stats characteristic`): means of yearly values by IMGW-PIB's calendar definitions, Tier B (CMP-1.1).

**What the cost has to fit into:**

- On request (a person's options), Obserwator runs at most two Hydrolog computations at a time, 30 s each, held in memory only (`gui/_analyses.py:1-15`, `TIMEOUT_S` `:52`). Requests wait in that queue: the public instance answered `GET /api/analysis/351220495/mann_kendall.temperature.frost_days?first_year=2010&last_year=2026` in 184.8 s on 2026-11-23 08:42 UTC, with six other on-request trends of the same station queued at 140–178 s (`~/.local/state/nogat/obserwator/logs/access-2026-11-23.jsonl`). `flood_frequency.Q` answered from the store in 0.06 s median (60 requests, 2026-11-14 to 24) and was computed on request in 2.4–3.4 s (9 requests on 2026-11-19 and 21: other distributions or fit methods); `precipitation_quantiles` on request took 13–15 s with another fit method and 31–65 s with the hydrological year (its maxima computed first).
- Stored: `analyses build` computed 5,561 stations (3,240 IMGW-PIB, 2,321 ČHMÚ), about 150,000 results, in 28,334 s with 3 workers on 2026-11-23 (`~/data/obserwator-310/build.log`, `out-analyses.json`); a new Hydrolog version computes every station again (`store/analyses.py:16-22`).
- A parametric bootstrap already runs in every L-moments fit: `ad_critical_values` from 999 samples drawn from the fitted distribution, each refitted by L-moments, seed 20261118 (`high_flows.py:64-69`, `:156-192`). Its ADR-0048 measured the refits: about 0.2 s for the four distributions by L-moments; about 0.15 s for one maximum-likelihood GEV, so 999 of them take minutes, and the moments solve the GEV shape numerically too.

**Display and consumers:**

- Obserwator's chart engine has `band` series, `[x, lo, hi]` filled between (`gui/web/src/charts/chart.js:24-26`), used by the regime and the low-flow deficit; flood and low-flow curves are drawn on its `gumbel` x axis (`sections/hydro/extremes.js:150-161`, `:232-251`); the quantile tables (`extremes.js:83-90`) and the figures „Q1% (T = 100)” and „NQ 10-letni” (`extremes.js:293`, `:191`).
- Exports: `obserwator-analyses-export/1` carries Hydrolog's `result` whole, and the CSV has one row per scalar leaf of it with a JSON Pointer `path` (`store/analyses.py:2284-2301`; ADR-016 D9, the attribution columns ADR-028), so new fields of the result reach both without code.
- `obserwator-analyses/1` holds the registry's digest, not results (`definitions`); `obserwator-station-analyses/1` holds per result its `options` and `formula_versions`; the result is Hydrolog's answer line byte for byte (`store/analyses.py:1-29`).
- Hydrolog's own GUI shows the quantiles as tiles and exports them as CSV (`gui/web/src/sections/statystyka/results-panel.js:6-8`, `:245-247`); its chart module has no fill-between series (`charts/chart.js:17-18` knows only a `band` y scale).
- Archiwista indexes stored analyses only as versions of the slot `analyses:<key>` and never reads a result (`adapter/obserwator/adapter.py:151-156`, `:391-432`). Hydrograf calls none of these commands.

## Methods [proposed]

One parameter for every interval: `confidence_level` c, the two-sided central interval, as `stats monthly` has it; default 0.95 (as `stats monthly` and Sen's `alpha` 0.05) [proposed, question 1]. KZGW's one-sided β upper limit is the upper limit of the central interval at c = 2β − 1: β = 84 % is c = 0.68, u_0.84 = 0.994 (KZGW 2017, eq. 3.12 and 3.26), so `confidence_level=0.68` gives KZGW's upper limit by KZGW's own rule for a maximum-likelihood fit (the table below) and the 84th percentile of the bootstrap for an L-moments fit: the same one-sided 84 % by another method.

Rejected: a bootstrap for every fit method (minutes per request, ADR-0048); the profile likelihood (Coles 2001, sect. 3.3.3), better than the delta method at large T but one constrained search per period, 25 per fit; a nonparametric bootstrap of the annual maxima (too narrow for 30–60 values, Kyselý 2008); reading KZGW's tables A.3 and A.4 into code: Hydrolog computes the information itself, and the printed σ of the examples serve as reference values only, so no published table enters the code (CMP-2.5 does not apply). The observed information of all three parameters of Pearson III and Weibull: with the bound estimated it is not regular below a shape of 2 (Smith 1985), where Hydrolog's search reaches (|γ| < 2, Weibull shape > 1, `frequency.py:1910-1915`) and typical Polish maxima lie (the L-moments skewness of KZGW's Polana, Rypin and Bukówka is 1.6–1.9), and it reproduces none of KZGW's examples; nor does Q_T ± zσ for the log-normal (6.95 against eq. 3.41's 7.05 m³/s in ex. 3.3). Where KZGW reads ε from a graph (ex. 3.2, 3.4) or from its quantile formula (eq. 3.36), Hydrolog holds its own estimate, and each case states KZGW's (CMP-2.3).

## Specs and the standard (CMP)

- **Nogat, standard 1.2 [proposed]:** CMP-2.3 adds to the conventions a spec lists „the side and confidence level of an interval and its method: the delta method with its information (observed or expected), its scale and the parameters it holds at their estimates; a bootstrap with its kind, sample count, seed and empirical-quantile rule; order statistics”. A new rule of CMP §6 after CMP-6.10, *Interval estimators*, numbered when it lands: „Besides its reference cases and oracles, an interval's coverage, the share of intervals that hold the true value, is simulated, seeded, from the distributions the formula fits, at the sample sizes of the ecosystem's use (30 and 60 values for annual series) with at least 1,000 samples per setting, nightly and before each release, never in the gate (as CMP-7.1), and reported per setting in `docs/FORMULAS.md`. A test asserts an exact method's coverage (order statistics, Student's t on normal samples) within the Monte Carlo error 2√(c(1 − c)/m) of its m samples, and an approximate method's as its source reports it for the setting, or above a stated bound with its reason (CMP-6.8). The generated properties are lower ≤ upper and the interval of a smaller c inside that of a larger one on the same sample.” *Why:* an interval can be right digit by digit and still not cover what it says; an approximate method's coverage at 30 values is the method's, not the code's, so it is reported, not forced to c. The reference module gains `assert_coverage` with its `reason` (CMP-6.8), tested in Nogat with a deliberately wrong interval (CMP-8.2); the copies of the module in Kartograf, Obserwator, Hydrolog, Hydrograf and Hydraulik are then behind until each project's next release copies it (CMP-8.1, `drift`). One ECO decision (the next free number at merge, ECO-085's rule) records the convention: intervals are computed by Hydrolog and shown by Obserwator, one two-sided `confidence_level`, the field names below, KZGW's rule for the maximum-likelihood fits it covers. The changelog of 1.2 in `standards/README.md` names CMP-2.3 and the new rule (HUB-6.1).
- **Hydrolog:** new Tier A specs, `formula_version` 1 each: `quantile_interval_bootstrap` (L-moments), `quantile_interval_delta` (ML, per distribution), `duration_curve_annual_median_interval`; `low_flow` uses the delta spec on negated minima. The point specs keep their versions, since no point result changes (CMP-2.8); a result's `formula_versions` names the interval specs it used. `docs/FORMULAS.md` and `formulas/<id>.toml` regenerated (CMP-2.6).

## The result shape [proposed]

Additions within `hydrolog-result/1` and its domain documents `hydrolog-stats-flood-frequency/1`, `hydrolog-stats-low-flow/1` and `hydrolog-stats-duration-curve/1`: optional fields only, so every version stays `/1` (AGI-4.1, ECO-085). Names as `stats monthly`'s:

```json
"confidence_level": 0.95,
"ci_method": "parametric_bootstrap",
"ci_lower": [12.1, 14.0, ...],
"ci_upper": [15.8, 19.6, ...],
"ci_samples": 997
```

- Per fit of `stats flood-frequency`; once in `stats low-flow`; in `stats duration-curve` beside `curve_values`, with the median annual curve as `annual_median_values`. `ci_lower` and `ci_upper` align with `quantiles` (or the curve's percentages); an element is `null` where undefined. `ci_method` is `parametric_bootstrap`, `delta` or `order_statistics`; `ci_samples` the bootstrap samples kept of 999, `null` otherwise.
- Not computed: `ci_method`, `ci_lower`, `ci_upper` and `ci_samples` `null`, `confidence_level` as asked, and the notice `interval_not_computed`, level `warning` as `single_year_month` (`catalog/notices.py:237-243`), with `details.reason` `fit_method` (moments, least squares), `too_few_values`, `bootstrap_failures` (fewer than 990 of 999 samples kept), `nonregular_fit` (a GEV with ξ ≤ −0.5) or `too_few_complete_years`. One bound `null` and the rest kept: `below_physical_range`, a bound of a discharge or a precipitation below 0 (CMP-3.4), naming its return periods.
- The option `confidence_level`, number in (0, 1), on the three commands, default 0.95, as `stats monthly`'s.
- `obserwator-analyses/1` changes only through the registry's digest; `obserwator-station-analyses/1` gains `confidence_level` in the `options` of these results and the interval specs in their `formula_versions`: new keys of open maps, additive. `obserwator-analyses-export/1` and its CSV carry the fields as rows beside `quantiles` without change.
- The consumers that read the fields are Obserwator and Hydrolog's GUI, both changed in this plan; Archiwista and Hydrograf do not read these results (above), so ECO-085's condition holds.

- **Options:** `confidence_level` overridable in the registry, chosen in the view as „Poziom ufności: 68 % / 90 % / 95 %”, the info text saying what the upper bound at 68 % is; a choice other than the stored one is computed on request, at today's cost (2.4–3.4 s for a flood frequency, 13–15 s for precipitation quantiles with another fit method, in the public logs of 2026-11-14 to 24).
- **Exports:** nothing to build; the manual (USAGE) names the new paths of the CSV.

## Open questions for the author (Fable's recommended answers under each; not decided)

1. Poziom ufności domyślny: 95 % dwustronnie (jak dziś średnie miesięczne i nachylenie Sena), czy górna granica wg KZGW 2017 przy β = 84 % (dwustronnie 68 %), której używa rozporządzenie ((1 + δ)Q_k) i z którą można porównać górne granice PMAXTP? Czy Obserwator ma przechowywać oba?
   Rekomendacja Fable: przechowywać 95 % dwustronnie, 68 % liczyć na żądanie, nie przechowywać obu. Przechowywane dopasowania są L-momentowe, więc ich granica przy 68 % nie byłaby granicą KZGW, którą daje metoda największej wiarygodności, liczona i tak na żądanie; drugi poziom podwoiłby przechowywane wyniki dla wartości, którą Obserwator liczy na żądanie w 2,4–3,4 s (przepływy) albo 13–15 s (opady). 95 % mają już średnie miesięczne i nachylenie Sena.
2. Metody momentów i najmniejszych kwadratów: bez przedziału (pole puste z komunikatem), czy bootstrap tylko na żądanie, kosztem minut na jedno obliczenie, ponad dzisiejsze 30 s Obserwatora?
   Rekomendacja Fable: bez przedziału, z komunikatem. Bootstrap dopasowań iteracyjnych to minuty (ADR-0048), ponad 30 s jednego obliczenia; obie metody są tylko na żądanie, a L-momenty (przechowywane) i największa wiarygodność (KZGW) przedział mają.
3. Krzywa czasu trwania: dodać obok krzywej z całego okresu medianową krzywą roczną Vogela i Fennessey z jej przedziałem (inna wielkość, wymaga pełnych lat), czy zostawić krzywą bez przedziału?
   Rekomendacja Fable: dodać. Przedział medianowej krzywej rocznej traktuje jako niezależne lata, nie dni (Vogel i Fennessey 1994), kosztuje milisekundy i wymaga tylko pełnych lat (6 przy 95 %); krzywa z całego okresu zostaje główną, druga krzywa z pasmem stoi obok niej.
4. SSQ, SWQ, SNQ: przedział t-Studenta średniej z wartości rocznych (tani, gotowa specyfikacja), czy zostają charakterystykami okresu wg IMGW-PIB, bez przedziału?
   Rekomendacja Fable: nie w tym planie; zostają charakterystykami okresu wg IMGW-PIB (Tier B, CMP-1.1). Przedział t-Studenta ma sens tylko dla trzech średnich z dwunastu wartości siatki (ZWQ, ZSQ, ZNQ to mediany, WWQ i NNQ ekstrema), a dołożyłby czwarty dokument (`hydrolog-stats-characteristic/1`) i zmianę tabeli „Charakterystyki”. Jeżeli tak, to dla wszystkich średnich z wartości rocznych polecenia (H, Q, T, rok i półrocza), nie tylko dla Q.
5. Wydania: przedziały w Hydrologu `0.10.0` razem z przypadkami CAMELS-PL, a w Obserwatorze w `v3.5.0` po `v3.4.0` z CAMELS-PL, czy wcześniej?
