## Design

| ID | Decision |
|---|---|
| B1 | **One catalogue, library-neutral.** `design/kit/nogat-basemaps.json`, data a server reads (its CSP, its regulamin's blocks, the check of its setting) and a page receives from its server: Hydrograf's set, ids, labels and order (`layers.js:62-67`). `openfreemap`, „OpenFreeMap szary”: vector, the TileJSON `https://tiles.openfreemap.org/planet` (Obserwator's and Archiwista's address; Hydrograf's `styles/positron` goes, B4), blocks `osm`. `gugik-osm`, „OSM GUGiK szary (tylko Polska)”: raster XYZ of `layers.js:22`, zoom 0–19, grey, bounded by the box of Poland as the orthophoto is (today unbounded), blocks `osm` and `gugik`. `gugik-ortho`, „Ortofotomapa GUGiK”: raster WMTS KVP of `layers.js:47-50`, zoom 0–19, bounds 13.8–24.4° E, 48.8–55.0° N, block `gugik`. Each entry: `id`, `label`, `kind` (`vector` or `raster`), `url`, `origin`, `minzoom`, `maxzoom`, `bounds`, `grey`, `default` (whether a GUI may open on it by default, B3: `false` for GUGiK's two entries, whose services are often unstable, Risks), `credit` (B5: its text and the links in it), `blocks`. „bez podkładu” is no entry: the switcher's last choice, always. No entry needs a key; no Esri (ECO-068); no topographic map (open question 5). |
| B2 | **One module in the kit, as two files.** `design/kit/nogat-basemaps.js` and `design/kit/nogat-basemaps.css`, a plain ES module that imports nothing (as every kit script, and as `plan/unified-charts` C1), so a GUI that loads a kit file by its hash (Hydrograf's `?v=`, `js/nogat.js`) meets no other version behind it; the map library is passed in, never imported. Exports: `BASEMAP_TEXTS`, the Polish words („Podkłady kartograficzne”, Hydrograf's group, `layers.js:765`; „Podkład kartograficzny”, its radio group's name, `:95`; „bez podkładu”; „Podkład nie odpowiada”, Archiwista's first words, `labels/map.js:24`), which a GUI in another language replaces with its own, labels included (Obserwator's English, as `spec.texts` of the chart module); `<nogat-basemaps>` (B3); `maplibreBasemap(entry, options)` → a Promise of `{sources, layers}` and `removeMaplibreBasemap(map)`, `repaintMaplibreBasemap(map, colors)` for a theme switch; `leafletBasemap(L, entry, options)` → a Promise of a Leaflet layer; both async, since a vector entry's TileJSON is fetched and each of its tile addresses checked to stay on the entry's origin before any tile is asked (Obserwator's `async function loadBasemapSource`, `map.js:139-152`), and both reject, after `onFailure(id)`, when it fails or leaves the origin; a source or layer carries the entry's credit (B5; a deployment's own base map the deployment's, B11) as its attribution, never the TileJSON's English one; `options` = `glyphs` (the GUI's vendored Noto Sans, as Obserwator and Archiwista ship), `locale` (names `name:pl`, else Latin, else local, after Archiwista's `style.js:50`; `name:en` first on an English page), `colors` (from the tokens, with the kit's light fallbacks where a GUI has no `tokens.css` copy yet, as Archiwista; `plan/unified-charts` C1), `before` (the GUI's lowest overlay, under which the base map goes, Obserwator's `OVERLAY`, `map.js:92`), `onFailure(id)`. |
| B3 | **The switcher, Hydrograf's.** `<nogat-basemaps>`: a radio list with `role="radiogroup"` named „Podkład kartograficzny”, the allowed entries in the catalogue's order, then the deployment's own base maps (B11), then „bez podkładu”; property `entries` (what the GUI's server allows, B6, and its own base maps, B11), `value`, event `change` with the id; an entry whose provider fails carries „Podkład nie odpowiada” beside it. A GUI places it where its layers are: Hydrograf's group „Podkłady kartograficzne” at the foot of „Warstwy”, its MZP row (an overlay, its own) staying above it; Archiwista's legend card in place of its `<select>`; Obserwator's map legend; Kartograf's map panel; on a phone the same list in the GUI's layers sheet. The default is the first allowed entry whose `default` is true (`openfreemap` with every entry allowed; never a GUGiK entry, Risks), else „bez podkładu”. A switch replaces the base map under the overlays and keeps the view (`layers.js:119-139`). The element remembers the choice in the browser (open question 4): `localStorage` key `nogat-basemap`, as the theme's `nogat-theme`, no server and no cookie; it opens on the remembered id while that id is allowed or names one of the deployment's own base maps (B11), else on the default. Archiwista in its team mode keeps each person's account preference instead (step 3). |
| B4 | **How each library draws an entry.** MapLibre: a vector entry is the kit's grey style over the OpenMapTiles schema, Obserwator's `basemapLayers` (`map.js:165-240`, itself after Archiwista's `style.js`), its colours from the tokens in both themes (DEV-15.3), its TileJSON's tiles checked to stay on the entry's origin (`map.js:139-152`), its glyphs the GUI's own; a raster entry a `raster` source with `bounds` and `maxzoom`, grey as `raster-saturation: -1` (`style.js:154`). Leaflet: a raster entry `L.tileLayer` with `bounds`, `maxZoom` and the kit's class `nogat-basemap-grey` (Hydrograf's `.basemap-grey`, `app.css:200`, moves into the kit's CSS); a vector entry `L.maplibreGL` with the style object `maplibreBasemap` builds, so Hydrograf draws OpenFreeMap as the other GUIs do (open question 2). `onFailure` fires when an entry's TileJSON or its first tiles fail (Obserwator's `sourcedata` check, `map.js:591-610`; Leaflet's `tileerror`); the map stays usable: the GUI keeps its own background and its overlays under it (Obserwator's borders), the element marks the entry, the GUI shows a notice on the map, and nothing switches by itself: the person picks another entry (Risks: GUGiK). Hydrograf's fallback to `gugik-osm` without MapLibre goes: MapLibre is vendored since its ADR-085. |
| B5 | **The credit.** The catalogue's, in the map's own attribution control, the corner the OpenStreetMap Foundation's attribution guideline names for a browsable map (https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Licence/Attribution_Guidelines, „Interactive maps”, read 2026-11-24) and DEV-20.9 already names for GUGiK, never in `<nogat-sources>`, whose lines are the data's (DEV-20.5): Obserwator's „Podkład mapy: …” leaves the strip for MapLibre's control. Wording (open question 3): `openfreemap` „OpenFreeMap © OpenMapTiles, dane © autorzy OpenStreetMap”, `gugik-osm` „© autorzy OpenStreetMap, GUGiK”, `gugik-ortho` „Ortofotomapa © GUGiK” (DEV-20.9), „OpenStreetMap” linked to https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright (the guideline: „Attribution must be to “OpenStreetMap”” and the link shows the ODbL), „OpenMapTiles” to https://openmaptiles.org/ (its design licence asks for it, https://github.com/openmaptiles/openmaptiles/blob/master/LICENSE.md), „OpenFreeMap” to https://openfreemap.org, „GUGiK” to https://www.geoportal.gov.pl. The kit's CSS, scoped under the class `nogat-basemap-map` that the adapters set on the map's container, draws both libraries' controls at the strip's 12 px in the muted text colour (Hydrograf's are 10 px, `app.css:224-231`). Up to 860 px the credit is shown when the map opens and folds behind „i” at the first drag, a tap showing it again: MapLibre 6.14.0's `compact` control does so (it opens with `maplibregl-compact-show` and folds on `drag`), and the Leaflet adapter does the same. The guideline lets a credit collapse „automatically on map interaction” or after five seconds, but „the attribution format should not require individuals to interact with the map or produced work to see the attribution”, so a credit folded from the start does not fit it. |
| B6 | **The setting `basemaps`.** The ids a deployment allows, a TOML list, shown in the catalogue's order; default every entry; `[]` none: no switcher, no base map, no request to a provider, no block for it. A shared key of `[nogat]`, overridden by `[<tool>] basemaps` and by `NOGAT_BASEMAPS` or `<TOOL>_BASEMAPS` (ids joined by `,`), as DEV-18.1 orders them; an unknown id stops the server at start with an error document naming it (`details.option`, `details.value`, as Obserwator's `bad_basemap_url`, `gui/_config.py:144-145`). Beside it `basemap_mirrors`, a deployment's own copy of an allowed entry (open question 9), and `basemap_extra`, a deployment's own base maps (B11). It replaces Archiwista's list of `BasemapConfig` as the list a GUI shows (its own base maps staying in its settings as `basemap_extra`, B11), Hydrograf's hard-coded list and Obserwator's `gui_basemap_url` and `gui_basemap_attribution` (amended 2026-11-24, open question 9: no deviation). Obserwator `v3.5.0` still accepts both, and `OBSERWATOR_GUI_BASEMAP_URL`, deprecated, and translates them at start: `none`, `off` or empty, an environment variable set but empty included, → `basemaps = []`; the default address `https://tiles.openfreemap.org/planet` → nothing; any other address → a copy of `openfreemap` in `basemap_mirrors`; `gui_basemap_attribution` is ignored, the catalogue giving the credit (B5). Either old key set logs a warning at start naming `basemaps` and `basemap_mirrors`; with an old and a new key both set, the new one wins and the warning says so. Its changelog lists both under „Deprecated”, and they go in Obserwator's next major, whenever it comes (none is planned), so REL-1.1 holds. Archiwista's `offline` stays its own: it also stops every other request outside Archiwista. |

## Risks

- **The kit draws OpenFreeMap itself** (open question 2). Its grey style reads OpenMapTiles' schema (3.16.0 in OpenFreeMap's TileJSON, read 2026-11-24; „The map schema is unmodified OpenMapTiles”, https://openfreemap.org), where Positron follows its provider; a change of the schema empties layers or names in every GUI at once. One fix in the kit, copied by each GUI; the browser tests' recorded tiles are renewed with each schema version.
- **No guarantee of service.** OpenFreeMap offers no SLA (https://openfreemap.org, read 2026-11-24); a failed entry says „Podkład nie odpowiada” and the switcher keeps the others (Archiwista's `docs/PROGRESS.md:207`: „one default provider (OpenFreeMap) is an availability risk outside offline mode”).
- **GUGiK's services are often unstable** (the author, 2026-11-24: „GUGiK okazuje się często być niestabilnym źródłem”). `gugik-osm` and `gugik-ortho` are never the default (B1, B3), so a GUI opens on a GUGiK entry only when a person chose it; when one fails, the map stays usable with the GUI's background and overlays, the switcher and a notice on the map say „Podkład nie odpowiada”, and the person switches (B4). Hydrograf's admin dialog, an operator's tool without a switcher, draws the first allowed raster entry (`gugik-osm` by default) and shows the same notice.
- **Copies.** Until its step a GUI keeps its own base maps under a recorded deviation and takes fixes only; `drift` names a copy of the module that lags (DEV-15.6).
