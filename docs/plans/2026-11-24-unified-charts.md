# Plan: one chart module in the kit — zoom, pan and reset alike in every GUI

Approved: 2026-11-24, the author (in session: „Wspólne wykresy: wszystko ok”)

Status: approved on 2026-11-24 with the author's answers to open questions 1 to 7, each as recommended (branch `plan/unified-charts`, not merged); nothing done. Reviewed by Fable on 2026-11-24 (`~/workspace/wt/logs/fable-unified-charts.md`): its decisions are applied below. The design's proposals, marked „[proposed]” in the draft, were approved with it.

Requirements: HUB-5.2, DEV-1.2, DEV-15.1, DEV-15.2, DEV-15.3, DEV-15.6, DEV-17.5, DEV-19.21, DEV-20.5, REL-1.4, ECO-055, ECO-058, ECO-072. Source: Obserwator's feedback #9 (2026-11-23): „Fajnie byłoby dodać zoom i przesuwanie do wykresów w tych wszystkich apkach. No i reset widoku po zoomie.” Related: the GUI platform plan `2026-11-01-nogat-gui-platform.md` (pending, deferred on 2026-11-12), whose stage 2 proposed a `chart-theme.js` that kept Chart.js beside the hand-drawn SVG; this plan replaces that row for charts.

## The author's direction (2026-11-24)

- „musimy ujednolicać pomiędzy projektami zachowanie i technologię”: one behaviour and one implementation of charts across the GUIs.
- The interaction sketch, approved:
  - a drag over the plot selects a range of x (kept);
  - Ctrl with the wheel zooms around the cursor; the wheel alone scrolls the page;
  - Shift with a drag, or a drag with the middle button, pans;
  - a double click, the key 0 or „Cały zakres” resets;
  - on a phone two fingers pinch and pan; one finger scrolls the page and reads values;
  - a small bar under a zoomed chart with „−”, „+”, „Cały zakres” and the range shown;
  - the y axis fits the visible part;
  - scatter charts (the rating curve, PMAXTP) zoom both axes by a rectangle;
  - a PNG exports what is visible, a CSV the whole series.

## Design

| ID | Decision |
|---|---|
| C1 | **One module in the kit, as two files.** `design/kit/nogat-chart.js` (`drawChart(container, spec)` and its handle, the scales, axes and series, the gestures, the range bar, `toPng` and the words `CHART_TEXTS`) and `design/kit/nogat-chart.css`, built from Obserwator's engine at `b5a1c1a` (`draw.js`, `axes.js`, `scale.js`, `series.js`, `svg.js`, `png.js`, `labels.js` and the chart rules of `css/charts.css`): one script and one stylesheet, as every element of the kit, whose scripts import nothing, so a GUI that loads a kit module by its hash (Hydrograf's `?v=`) never meets a module of another version behind it. A plain ES module without Preact or a build step (DEV-15.1) that imports nothing of a GUI: its words come in `spec.texts` and its language in `spec.locale` (C4), where Obserwator's engine reads `i18n.js`. Its CSS is the kit's kind: every token with its light fallback (`var(--info, …)`, as `nogat-sources.css`), so a GUI without `tokens.css` (Archiwista) draws it in the light values (DEV-15.3); its container is `.nogat-chart`, the classes outside it carry that prefix (`nogat-chart-legend`, `nogat-chart-zoom`) and the rules inside it are scoped by it, the derived colours (`--series-1` to `--series-6`, `--rain`) set on it, not on `:root`, so it meets none of a GUI's own classes (Hydrograf's `.chart`, `.chart-legend`). Listed in `nogat-kit.json` and copied byte for byte (DEV-15.6); `drift` names a copy that lags. Each GUI keeps a wrapper of its own of some 20 lines (Obserwator's and Hydrolog's `Chart` for Preact, Archiwista's for Preact, Hydrograf's for its scripts through `js/nogat.js`). Series classes of one view (PMAXTP's ramp, the rating curve's fit) stay in the GUI's CSS. |
| C2 | **What it takes from Hydrolog's copy.** A short time axis labelled in minutes (`d33b2a2`); `toPng` with the providers' lines under the image (DEV-20.5). Hydrolog's `labels` becomes Obserwator's `tickList`. |
| C3 | **What it gains for Archiwista and Hydrograf.** A second unit on a right-hand axis (`y.side: "right"`, beside `y2`, the panel above); `span` marks (a grey band over a gap); point shapes `hollow` and `diamond`; an x axis drawn without its labels, so Archiwista's compare panels keep one time axis under the last; `onReadout(index | null)`, so Hydrograf's profile moves its map marker; `onRange({min, max, width} | null)` once a range settles (the end of a drag, a pinch or a key, the wheel idle 200 ms) and the handle's `plotWidth()`, `width` the plot's width in CSS pixels, so Archiwista asks for the range at that many points (`max_points`) and keeps `from` and `to` in its address; `zoom.set` and `zoom.reset` take a range back and never call `onRange`. Charts that show one range share it through their GUI, by `onRange` and `zoom.set` (Archiwista's compare panels through the address, as today); the module keeps no state across charts. |
| C4 | **Words and language.** The module's own words in Polish in an exported `CHART_TEXTS`, as the kit's other modules (`TEXTS` of `nogat-handoff.js`, `SOURCES_TEXTS` of `nogat-sources.js`); a GUI with another language passes its own through `spec.texts` (Obserwator from its `charts.*` catalog) and its language through `spec.locale` (`pl-PL` by default, Obserwator's `en-GB` in English), which writes every number and date of the ticks, the readout and the bar (a decimal comma or point, month names, a day as dd.mm.rrrr or dd/mm/rrrr, through `Intl`), and redraws on its language switch with `update`. Dates in each GUI's own format, passed in the spec where it differs from the locale's (Archiwista's `RRRR.MM.DD`, its ADR-016 of 2026-10-17), so a GUI's charts, tables and cards write a day one way (open question 7). One word for the reset everywhere: „Cały zakres” (Obserwator's „Pokaż całość” and the „Cały okres” of Archiwista's chart go; the kit's „Cały okres” of `<nogat-admin-stats>`, a period of the statistics, not a reset, stays). |
| C5 | **Mouse and trackpad.** A drag selects x (a rectangle on a `zoom: "xy"` chart) when it covers at least 6 units of the viewBox (Obserwator's `DRAG`), else it is a click. Ctrl with the wheel zooms around the cursor, the x under the cursor staying there, by 1.25 per notch (Archiwista's) and in proportion for a trackpad's small steps, and prevents the browser's page zoom over that chart only; the wheel alone is never taken, the page scrolls. Chromium and Firefox deliver a trackpad's pinch as wheel events with `ctrlKey`, and Safari does since version 15 (https://danburzo.ro/dom-gestures/, read 2026-11-24), so a pinch over the chart zooms it. Shift with a drag, or a drag with the middle button (its autoscroll prevented), pans, held within the whole. A double click resets. Every zoom in (a drag, a rectangle, Ctrl+wheel, a pinch, `+`) stops at 4 points of the readout's series in the range (Obserwator's limit, today on `+` only), on a chart without a readout series at a thousandth of the whole (of each axis of a `zoom: "xy"` chart); panning stops at the whole's edges. A burst of wheel, pinch or pan events redraws once per animation frame, the moves in between applied together, so a long series (ČHMÚ's since 1775) follows the fingers. |
| C6 | **Phone.** A zoomable plot has `touch-action: pan-y`: one finger moving up or down scrolls the page, one finger along the plot moves the readout (today a touch reads out where it lands and a drag scrolls the page, `draw.js:500-503`), two fingers pinch (around their middle) and pan. A drag of one finger never selects a range. The browser's own pinch zoom of the page is not available over a zoomable chart; outside it, it is. Up to 860 px a zoomable chart is drawn at its container's width (as `fit`, at its size's ratio), never at the 560 px minimum of a wide chart that scrolls sideways (Obserwator's `css/charts.css:188-190`, Hydrolog's `:159-160`): `pan-y` leaves no finger to scroll it, and a pinch shows any part larger. A chart that does not zoom keeps the browser's touch and that minimum. |
| C7 | **The range bar.** Under a zoomed chart, one line at 390 px: „−” („Oddal”), „+” („Przybliż”), „Cały zakres”, and the range shown („{from} – {to}” in the axis's format; a `zoom: "xy"` chart names x and y). Buttons at least 24 × 24 px (WCAG 2.2 SC 2.5.8), the identity's `.btn.sm` (`design/README.md`, Components), which every GUI's own CSS defines 26 px high. A zoomable chart not yet zoomed shows a lone „+” in the bar's place, which zooms by a tap around the readout or the middle (open question 4); a reset brings the lone „+” back. |
| C8 | **Axes.** On a `zoom: true` chart the y axis (and `y2`, a right-hand axis) fits the values within the range shown (Obserwator's `extents` already does so). A `zoom: "xy"` chart zooms and pans x and y alike, by a rectangle, Ctrl+wheel, pinch and keys; its y is then the person's, not fitted. `zoom: "xy"`: Obserwator's rating curve (both charts) and PMAXTP; others where a GUI asks for it. |
| C9 | **Which charts zoom.** By default every chart whose x is `date`, `datetime`, `time`, `linear` or `log`; never a `band` y or a share bar. A GUI sets `zoom: false` on a chart of a year's months or days (the regime, month climatologies), where a range means nothing, and on a Gumbel (probability) plot of Obserwator and Hydrolog: tens of annual values on a probability axis with fixed ticks, each read by the readout (open question 5). |
| C10 | **Keys** (the chart is focusable where it reads out or zooms, as Obserwator's): arrows read the values, Shift or Page ten steps, Home and End the first and last, Esc hides the readout (Obserwator's); `+` and `−` zoom around the readout or the middle, the arrows past the range's edge move it a quarter on, `0` resets. Archiwista's `Home` reset becomes `0` and its arrows read out. |
| C11 | **Accessibility.** `role="img"` with the chart's title, an `aria-description` naming the keys and the gestures, an `aria-live="polite"` region that says the range after each zoom (Obserwator's `keysHint`, `draw.js:358-411`); the bar's buttons named; every gesture has a key and a button that does the same, the lone „+” of C7 before the first zoom among them, so no zoom needs a path or two fingers (WCAG 2.2 SC 2.5.1, 2.5.7). Both themes from tokens. |
| C12 | **Exports.** `toPng` draws the chart as shown: the range shown, the y fitted, without the readout and the selection (Obserwator's `svgText`), with the providers' lines under it (Hydrolog's footer, DEV-20.5); the file names stay each GUI's (DEV-19.21). It takes any drawn SVG, as Hydrolog's takes Sieć's network schematic, and marks it with the class `nogat-chart-exporting` while it reads the styles (Hydrolog's `.schem.exporting` rules follow in step 3). A CSV holds the whole series whatever the range shown; the kit writes no CSV. A hand-off is not an export: Archiwista's keeps the chart's range (its D4 erratum). |
| C13 | **The range across redraws.** Kept while the data are the same (Obserwator's `sameData`), reset when they change; a GUI may keep it elsewhere through `onRange` and `zoom.set` (Archiwista's address). |
| C14 | **Hydrograf off Chart.js** (option A below, open question 3): its six charts are drawn by the kit's module; the land cover and the HSG, today one stacked bar each beside a class list, as the `band` y: one bar per class with its share, as Hydrolog's Kalkulatory, a change of their look that step 5's ADR records; the peaks as `peak` marks, the profile's marker through `onReadout`; the theme stylers go (colours are CSS classes); Chart.js 4.4.7 and its licence leave the vendor directory. |
| C15 | **The rule**, a new rule of DEV §15 (its next free number, 15.15 on `develop` today), written in step 1 with the answers to open questions 4 and 5 (a lone „+” before a zoom; probabilities among the exceptions): „A GUI draws its charts over axes with the kit's chart module (`nogat-chart.js` and `nogat-chart.css`, DEV-15.6), never with a chart library or a drawing of its own; a figure the module does not draw (a circular chart, warming stripes) MAY be the GUI's own. A chart whose x is a time or a value shows a range of it, not a chart of a year's months or days, of named bars or of probabilities: a drag over the plot selects the range of x, of x and y on a chart of points; Ctrl with the wheel zooms around the cursor and the wheel alone scrolls the page; Shift with a drag, or a drag with the middle button, pans; a double click, the key 0 and „Cały zakres” show the whole. On a touch screen two fingers pinch and pan, one finger scrolls the page and reads the values. A chart not yet zoomed shows „+” under it; a zoomed chart shows under it „−”, „+”, „Cały zakres” and the range shown, and its y axis fits the values shown. A PNG of a chart is the chart as shown with the lines of DEV-20.5; a CSV holds the whole series. *Why:* one person moves between the GUIs, and four implementations had three sets of gestures (ECO-0NN).” |

### Hydrograf: two ways weighed

| | A: the kit's module (chosen) | B: an interim layer on Chart.js |
|---|---|---|
| Gestures | the same code as the other GUIs | `chartjs-plugin-zoom` 3.0.2 (npm registry, 2026-11-23; 3.0.0 of 2026-11-21 dropped the hammerjs dependency of 2.2.0) gives a drag zoom, a wheel zoom with `modifierKey: "ctrl"`, a pan with `modifierKey: "shift"`, pinch, and sets `touch-action: pan-y` when only x zooms (https://www.chartjs.org/chartjs-plugin-zoom/latest/guide/options.html, read 2026-11-24); the middle button, the keys, the range bar, the double click, the readout by keys and the live region are Hydrograf's own code |
| Look, words, PNG | the kit's: tokens as classes, `CHART_TEXTS`, `toPng` with the lines | Chart.js's canvas, restyled by stylers; a PNG of its own |
| Tests | the kit's browser tests, plus Hydrograf's e2e of its views | a second set for the same gestures |
| Cost | six chart functions (888 lines) rewritten on the kit's spec; Chart.js removed | a vendored plugin whose 3.0 line is three days old; later a second migration (the chart rule of C15) |

B is only worth it if Hydrograf must zoom before the kit's module exists; A is chosen (open question 3).
